Common questions

How do I place an order?

Message us on WhatsApp with the products you want. We confirm price, stock and delivery, then share payment details.

Are the products genuine?

Yes. We source from the brands and their distributors. Ask us if you'd like details on where a particular product comes from.

Which polish and pad should I use?

It depends on your paint hardness, the defects and your machine. Send us your car model and a photo of the paint and we'll suggest a combination.

How long does delivery take?

We dispatch in 1 to 2 working days. Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 working days. See Shipping & returns.

Can I return a product?

Unopened items can be returned within 7 days. Damaged or wrong items are always covered. Full details are on our Shipping & returns page.
