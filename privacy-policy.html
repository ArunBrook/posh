Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

This policy explains what personal information Posh Imperial ("we", "us") collects when you use this website or contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have. We handle personal data in line with applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Information we collect

  • Details you give us: your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, car model and the message you send when you enquire or order, whether through the contact form, WhatsApp, phone or email.
  • Order information: the products you order, payment confirmation details and delivery information.
  • Technical data: basic information such as browser type and pages visited, which our website host may log for security and performance.

The contact form on this website does not store your details. It prepares a message that opens in WhatsApp or your email app, and you choose whether to send it.

How we use your information

  • To reply to your enquiries and recommend products.
  • To process, deliver and support your orders, including returns.
  • To keep records required for accounting and tax.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We only use your details for these purposes. We do not sell your personal information.

Consent

By contacting us or placing an order, you agree that we may use your information as described here. You can withdraw consent at any time by contacting us, though we may need to keep certain records where the law requires it.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed with service providers who help us fulfil your order, such as courier companies and payment providers. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also handled by WhatsApp under its own terms and privacy policy.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will update this policy.

Third-party websites

Our website may link to other sites, such as social media, maps and fonts. We are not responsible for their privacy practices.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry and order details only as long as needed to serve you and to meet legal and accounting requirements, then delete or anonymise them.

Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, or to delete it. You may also ask us to stop contacting you. Send your request to the contact details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Children

Our products and website are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
