Shipping & returns

Last updated: 21 September 2026

Dispatch

We usually dispatch orders within 1 to 2 working days of confirmed payment. We will share tracking details once your order ships.

Delivery

Delivery usually takes 3 to 7 working days depending on location. Remote areas may take longer. Delivery charges, if any, are confirmed before you pay.

Some chemicals, such as flammable or pressurised products, may have shipping restrictions. If your order includes one, we will tell you before dispatch.

Damaged or wrong items

Please record a short unboxing video when you open your parcel. If an item arrives damaged, leaking or incorrect, contact us within 7 days of delivery with the video and photos. We will replace the item or refund you.

Returns

  • Unopened products in original condition and packaging can be returned within 7 days of delivery.
  • Opened or used chemicals, coatings, polishes, applicators and pads cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons, unless faulty.
  • Machines and electrical tools carry the manufacturer's warranty. We will help you claim it.
  • Return shipping for change-of-mind returns is paid by the customer.

Refunds

Approved refunds are made to your original payment method within 7 to 10 working days after we receive and check the returned item.

Cancellations

You can cancel an order before it ships and receive a full refund. Once it has shipped, the return rules above apply.

Contact

To report a problem or start a return, message us on WhatsApp or email .
